# Prime 60 social content pack: whiteboard infographics

For Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Built for Blotato's **Whiteboard Infographic** template
(`ae868019-820d-434c-8fe1-74c9da99129a`), which takes two fields:

- **Infographic Description** (10 to 500 characters): what to draw.
- **Footer CTA Text** (2 to 100 characters): the line along the bottom.

Every prompt below fits those limits. Paste the description, paste the footer, generate.
The same descriptions also work in the **Classroom Chalkboard** template for variety.

Rules that keep the brand consistent:

- One idea per image. If it needs more than five boxes, it is a carousel, not an infographic.
- Numbers and lists come from the app, so a man who signs up finds exactly what the picture promised.
- No shame words. Never "failure", "lazy", "excuses". Use "return", "reset", "evidence".
- Footer always names the offer and the address.

Footer options (pick one per post):

1. `Free for the first 100 men. prime60.colindesilva.com`
2. `Prime 60: five minutes a day. Free for life for 100 founding members.`
3. `Save this. Then take your founding place at prime60.colindesilva.com`

## Captions, by platform

Hormozi's content unit is hook, retain, reward. The infographic is the retain; the caption supplies the hook and the reward (the free offer).

**Instagram (carousel or single image, 4:5).** Hook line first, then two or three short lines, then one call to action. Hashtags in the first comment, not the caption. Suggested set: `#over50 #menshealth #midlife #secondhalf #habits #atomichabits #prime60 #buildtheman`

Example, for infographic 6:

> Missing a day did not break your streak. Missing the next one does.
>
> One miss is an event. Two can become a pattern. The skill is not perfection. It is how fast you come back.
>
> Prime 60 measures exactly that, in five minutes a day. The first 100 men are free for life. Link in bio.

**TikTok (9:16; use the image as a 7-second slide, or hold it on screen while you talk to camera).** On-screen text uses the hook. Spoken script, 20 to 30 seconds:

> "If you are over 50 and you have started and quit the same plan more than twice, this is why. [point to loop] You measured the gym. You did not measure the coming back. One miss is an event. Two is a pattern. I built a five-minute tool that measures the return, not the streak. Free for the first 100 men. Link in bio."

**Facebook (single image, longer caption; your community already knows you).** Lead with a personal line, then the lesson, then the offer:

> I spent decades measuring one thing: work. Everything else got what was left over. Here is the scorecard I wish someone had handed me at 50. Rate yourself honestly. Then, if you want the daily version, I have made Prime 60 free for life for the first 100 men. prime60.colindesilva.com

## Cadence

Hormozi's Rule of 100, adapted: **one infographic post a day across all three platforms for 30 days**, with the caption adjusted per platform as above. Twelve infographics, each posted with two different hooks, covers 24 days. Fill the rest with the quote-card template and screenshots from your own daily use of the app.

Every post has one destination: the letter at prime60.colindesilva.com/letter.

## Producing them in Blotato

1. Go to my.blotato.com, Create, Visuals, choose **Whiteboard Infographic**.
2. Paste a description from above into Infographic Description.
3. Paste a footer into Footer CTA Text.
4. Generate. Check the text is legible and nothing is misspelt; regenerate if a word is garbled, which happens with AI images.
5. Save to your Blotato library and schedule the three platform versions with the captions above.

Variations worth testing after the first week: the **Classroom Chalkboard** template with the same descriptions; the **Quote Card with Paper Background and Highlight** template for the hook bank (its built-in title style, "I'm 35. If you're in your 30s or 40s, read this", becomes "I'm 61. If you're in your 50s or 60s, read this").
